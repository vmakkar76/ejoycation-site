How are the words selected? ▼

All 200 core vocabulary words are carefully selected from analysis of recent CEM, GL, and ISEB 11+ exam papers. We identified words that appear most frequently to ensure maximum relevance for exam success.

Will the cards last with regular use? ▼

Yes! The cards are made from high-quality, durable card stock and secured with metal rings. They're designed to withstand regular, daily use. Many customers report their sets lasting through multiple children.

How do these flashcards help with 11+ exam success? ▼

Strong vocabulary is essential for success in 11+ English exams (verbal reasoning, reading comprehension, and creative writing). These cards build comprehensive word knowledge through multiple learning angles: definitions, synonyms, antonyms, and contextual sentences. The portable design allows frequent, bite-sized practice.

How long should my child use the flashcards each day? ▼

Even just 10-15 minutes daily is effective! The beauty of these cards is their portability—just pull out a few cards and study during car journeys, lunch breaks, or morning routines. Consistency matters more than duration. Many parents keep a selection on a key ring for spontaneous review sessions.

Can I add my own words to the cards? ▼

Absolutely! The pack includes blank cards specifically for this purpose. As your child progresses, they can add their own challenging words, weak spots from practice tests, or new vocabulary they encounter in their reading.
